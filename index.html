02 / Operating system

Curious enough to
go all the way down.

I learn by building at the edge of my current ability — then I keep going until the thing works for someone besides me.

From broadcast rooms to production software.

By day, I produce broadcast video for Metro Nashville. That gives me a useful bias: I notice friction, I care about the operator, and I know the difference between an impressive prototype and a tool that survives contact with a deadline.

That mindset shows up in everything I make — APIs with failure modes, UIs with a clear next action, and systems that can run unattended without pretending the hard parts don't exist.
